# Data

Public data series used in the figures. Each file is used as downloaded.

| File | Series | Source | Retrieved |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `fred/UNRATE.csv` | US unemployment rate, monthly, seasonally adjusted | Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED), series `UNRATE`, from BLS. https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/UNRATE | 2026-11-12 |
| `fred/PRS85006173.csv` | Nonfarm business sector: labor share, index 2017 = 100, quarterly | FRED series `PRS85006173`, from BLS. https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/PRS85006173 | 2026-11-12 |
| `fred/LNS12300060.csv` | Employment-population ratio, ages 25 to 54, monthly | FRED series `LNS12300060`, from BLS. https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/LNS12300060 | 2026-11-12 |
| `fred/CIVPART.csv` | Labor force participation rate, monthly | FRED series `CIVPART`, from BLS. https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CIVPART | 2026-11-12 |

Smaller tables that were transcribed from published sources (horse numbers, farm
labor shares, benchmark results and so on) are kept next to the figure scripts
in `models/`, with their sources named in the script and in the research notes.
